# Real-World Data Project: Retail Business Analytics

## Overview
An end-to-end retail analytics project focused on sales, profitability, customer experience, regional performance, and operational efficiency.

## Business Questions
- Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which regions and sales channels perform best?
- How do discounts affect profit margins?
- What are the monthly revenue and profit trends?
- Is delivery time associated with customer ratings?
- Which payment methods are most common?

## Technologies
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset
The dataset includes:
- Order ID and date
- Region and sales channel
- Product category
- Payment method
- Units sold and unit price
- Discount
- Customer rating
- Delivery days
- Inventory available
- Gross sales, net sales, cost, profit, and profit margin

## Analysis Included
- Data-quality assessment
- Missing-value handling
- Duplicate removal
- Business KPI calculation
- Category, regional, and channel analysis
- Monthly revenue and profit trend
- Discount and profitability analysis
- Customer experience analysis
- Correlation heatmap
- Business recommendations

## How to Run
Open `real_world_retail_data_project.ipynb` in Google Colab, Jupyter Notebook, JupyterLab, or VS Code and run all cells.
